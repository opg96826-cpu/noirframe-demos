Our Story

Born from hunger.
Raised on flavour.

Selera means appetite — and ours is endless. We started with a single clay oven, family recipes passed down three generations, and one stubborn belief: great food should make you close your eyes on the first bite.

From slow-steamed dum biryanis to kebabs kissed by real charcoal, every plate at Selera is built the old way — no shortcuts, no compromises. The doors aren't open yet. But the fire is already lit.
