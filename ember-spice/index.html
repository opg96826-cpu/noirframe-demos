The Dum

Sealed At Dusk, Opened At Your Table.

Every handi starts the night before. Meat rests in yoghurt and red chilli, basmati is washed until the water runs clear, and whole spices are toasted until the kitchen smells like a wedding.

Then we layer, seal the lid with dough, and leave it alone on a low fire. The handi is never opened in the kitchen — it is opened at your table, and never before.

01

Marinate

Overnight in yoghurt, red chilli and ground spices.

02

Fire The Tandoor

Charcoal lit at dawn, burning clean by service.

03

Layer

Meat, rice, saffron milk and fried onion, in order.

04

Seal & Slow-Cook

Dough-sealed lid, low fire, no peeking.

05

Open At The Table

The first steam belongs to the guest, not the kitchen.
