Mine to masterpiece in five days

Every piece follows the same week, which is why we sell out by Saturday and never finish stock ahead.

01
Sunday

Sourced and shipped

Pearls leave Palawan on the ferry. Gold travels down from Benguet by bus.

02
Monday, 5am

Melted low

Gold arrives from the refiner and is melted gently the same morning.

03
Monday night

Set overnight

The piece rests for twelve hours with stones set by hand, one at a time.

04
Tuesday

Finished twelve at a time

Small batches, so the bench is never fuller than it should be. Cards are hand-written with the source.

05
Wednesday to Saturday

At your door in its box

Our own rider, across Metro Manila. Presented in its keepsake box, ready to wear.
